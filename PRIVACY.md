# Privacy

Roamling has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your walks, routes, steps, companions, finds and letters stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on.

**Location** is used to lift the fog where you walk, to know the district you are in and to show the weather. It is read only while the app is open, during a walk (with a notification on screen), and once at setup. Routes are never sent anywhere.

**What goes online, and only that:**
- map tiles from OpenStreetMap, for the part of the map on screen;
- the weather from Open-Meteo, for your rough position (three decimals, about 100 m);
- what a district is like and which places are near (parks, water, fountains, bridges) from the OpenStreetMap Overpass service, for a square of about a kilometre around you.

None of these requests carries anything about you; they are cached on the phone.

**Steps** come from the phone's step sensor during a walk. If you turn on Health Connect in the settings, the app also reads the steps your watch or band counted during your walks – never the rest of your day.

Notifications (eggs, wild companions, a rainbow, the step goal) are local; nothing is sent to show them.

Lingvale, the village of our pet apps, can read the companions and the days through a small read-only bridge. It answers only Lingvale signed with our key, changes nothing, and the data still never leaves the phone.
